# TrailSynth (web)

Little hikers walk a real piece of the Earth and play music from the ground under their feet: its height, slope, tiredness, land type and
temperature. Runs in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari; MIDI output needs Chrome or Edge). Nothing to install.

**Try it:** https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/trailsynth/

- The hikers, their notes and all the sounds are made live in your browser; there are no recordings.
- Maps and land cover come from OpenStreetMap and the AWS Terrain Tiles; weather from Open-Meteo. See **About** in the app for credits.
- Send the notes out as MIDI (Chrome/Edge): pick an output under **Sound > MIDI out**.

This repository holds only the built website. The desktop app (with virtual MIDI ports and plugin builds) is developed separately.
